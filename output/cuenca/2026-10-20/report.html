C. Compared with normal

Model-estimated rain over these 7 days: 36.1 mm. The 1991–2020 average for the same dates is 29.6 mm (122% of normal), and the middle half of years fell between 17.7 mm and 43.7 mm. That puts this week within the normal range: wetter than 21 of 30 years in the reference period.

Caution: both numbers come from weather models, and they come from two different models (IFS for this week, ERA5 for the normal). For Cuenca both models give roughly 1,800–2,000 mm of rain a year, about twice the 800–900 mm usually published for the city. Read rain amounts as relative ("wetter or drier than the model's normal"), not as exact millimetres.

E. Data sources

  • Forecasts: Open-Meteo Forecast API and Previous Runs API (model: best_match). "24 h ahead" means the value the model predicted 24 hours before each hour, not one specific published forecast.
  • Past weather: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API. For recent days this is the ECMWF IFS model, about 9 km resolution.
  • Normals: ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus Climate Change Service), 1991–2020, ~25 km resolution.
  • Airport observations: METAR reports from SECU via Iowa Environmental Mesonet.
  • Location: -2.9001, -79.0059 (central Cuenca), time zone America/Guayaquil. Weather can differ a lot across the city.

ERA5 reanalysis arrives about 5–7 days late, so recent days use the IFS model. On 7 overlapping days the two models' rain totals were 36.1 mm (IFS) vs 8.6 mm (ERA5).

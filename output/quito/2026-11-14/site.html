What this means for Quito: El Niño mainly brings heavy rain to Ecuador's coast. In the Andes its effect is weaker and less predictable, so an unusually wet or dry month in Quito is not, by itself, proof that El Niño caused it.

Back home in the US and Canada: El Niño winters usually bring more rain and cooler weather across the southern US, from California to Florida, and milder, drier weather to the northern US and western Canada. El Niño years also tend to have fewer Atlantic hurricanes. These are typical patterns, not guarantees; see NOAA's seasonal outlook for the current forecast.
